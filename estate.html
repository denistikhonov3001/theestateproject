The Estate and Park

A large coastal palace and park.

The same palace should keep the same architecture in every image. Around it: parkland, forest, arrival roads and an open coastline.

About the project↓Next steps→

All views should show the same palace and the same grounds.

The main idea

One palace. One consistent design.

First, the exterior of the building and the layout of the grounds should be fixed. New images should then show the same palace without random changes.

Main views

Palace and grounds

A few clear views are enough to start: the main façade, the park and the arrival route.

What should stay consistent

Three practical rules.

01

Consistent architecture

The façade, windows, roof, wings and main proportions should not change from image to image.

02

A planned park

Paths, avenues, trees, lawns and gathering areas should be designed together with the palace.

03

A coastal setting

The coast and sea horizon are part of the estate landscape.

The coast

The park reaches the sea.

Instead of an artificial pond, the project uses the real coastline, open water and the natural horizon.

Questions and answers

The basics, in plain language.

What exists today?

The Estate Project already exists as a public concept-development project: this website, defined project directions, visual references, written roadmaps and ongoing research. Physical construction has not started.

Is this a finished technical design?

No. The visuals are concept and reference material; feasibility and engineering would require qualified specialists.

How should the images on this page be understood?

They are visual references and concept images. They show the direction, not a finished project.

Is this an investment offer?

No. The site is a concept presentation and does not offer securities or promise returns.

What does voluntary support purchase?

Nothing is purchased. Support is a voluntary contribution to ongoing concept development and does not provide goods, services, membership, access, ownership or a guaranteed reward.

Does support guarantee construction?

No. Support does not guarantee that either concept will be physically built, nor does it promise a start date, completion date, budget or final technical solution.
