Voluntary support

Support ongoing concept development.

Support is optional. It helps with documented project-development costs and does not purchase a product, service, ownership interest or financial return.

What support may help fund

Website and domain costs, research and source material, visualisation, documentation, specialist consultations, concept-development tools, provider fees and preparation for later feasibility work.

What support does not provide

No goods or services, project share, equity, voting right, revenue share, repayment claim, investment return, guaranteed reward, construction date or completion promise.

Before contributing

Read the Support Policy and Mistaken Payment / Refund Policy. The external payment provider may apply its own fees, supported currencies, network conditions and transaction rules.
