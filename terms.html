Terms of Use

1. Project and operator

The Estate Project is an independent personal concept-development project operated through this public website. The site documents two separate long-term concepts and does not state that physical construction or shipbuilding has begun.

2. Informational and concept purpose

The website provides project information, research notes, references, visual concepts, status information and development roadmaps. These materials are not final architectural, engineering, naval, construction, financial or regulatory documents.

3. No sale on this website

The website itself does not sell goods, services, memberships, project shares or access rights. Voluntary support is handled by an external provider and is additionally described by the Support Policy and Mistaken Payment / Refund Policy.

4. No investment or financial product

Nothing on the website is an offer of securities, equity, partnership, debt, profit sharing, repayment rights or investment return. Voluntary support creates no ownership or financial rights in the project.

5. External services

The site may link to third-party services for communication, hosting, source material or voluntary support. Those services operate under their own terms, privacy policies, fees and technical rules.

6. Intellectual property and historical sources

Original project text, structure and presentation remain subject to their authors’ rights. Historical and third-party reference material remains subject to its identified source and licence terms. Source links are provided where applicable.

7. Accuracy and evolving status

The project is evolving. Information is published in good faith to describe the current concept-development stage, but future design, feasibility, costs, schedules, locations and implementation may change after specialist review.

8. Changes and contact

These terms may be updated as the project develops. The published version and date shown on the site are the current reference. Questions may be sent to the project contact email.
