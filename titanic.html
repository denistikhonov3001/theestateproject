RMS TITANIC · 1912
Full-size Titanic concept

Titanic with a historic appearance and modern systems.

The idea is to reproduce the ship’s exterior and selected famous interiors as accurately as practical, while using modern safety, navigation, accessibility and propulsion systems.

The main idea

Historic appearance. Modern operation.

Period photographs and documents are used to guide the exterior and interiors. Modern technical systems are designed separately so they do not spoil the historic appearance.

Questions and answers

The basics, in plain language.

What exists today?

The Estate Project already exists as a public concept-development project: this website, defined project directions, visual references, written roadmaps and ongoing research. Physical construction has not started.

Is this a finished technical design?

No. The visuals are concept and reference material; feasibility and engineering would require qualified specialists.

How should the images on this page be understood?

They are visual references and concept images. They show the direction, not a finished project.

Is this an investment offer?

No. The site is a concept presentation and does not offer securities or promise returns.

What does voluntary support purchase?

Nothing is purchased. Support is a voluntary contribution to ongoing concept development and does not provide goods, services, membership, access, ownership or a guaranteed reward.

Does support guarantee construction?

No. Support does not guarantee that either concept will be physically built, nor does it promise a start date, completion date, budget or final technical solution.
