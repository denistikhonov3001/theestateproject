What it is

The Estate Project is an independent public concept-development project documenting two separate long-term ideas: a coastal palace-and-park estate and a full-size Titanic reconstruction concept.

What it is not

It is not a completed architectural, naval or engineering project. Physical construction and shipbuilding have not started, and no construction date, budget or completion outcome is promised.

Titanic direction

The concept aims for a historically recognizable exterior and selected interiors while treating modern safety, navigation, accessibility, propulsion and operation as separate professional workstreams.

The Estate direction

The Estate focuses on a consistent palace, park, arrival sequence, forest and coastline, with an emphasis on maintaining the same architecture across future visualisations.

Independence

The project does not claim affiliation with any Titanic-related company, museum, estate, rights holder or official organization.

Support

Support is voluntary and does not provide equity, ownership, voting rights, repayment claims, profit share, investment returns or a guaranteed construction outcome.

Short description

The Estate Project is an independent public concept-development project exploring a coastal estate and a full-size Titanic reconstruction with historic character and modern systems.
