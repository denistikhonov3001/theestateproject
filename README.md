# The Estate Project

Independent public concept-development project documenting two separate long-term directions:

- **The Estate** — a large coastal estate and park concept.
- **Titanic** — a full-size reconstruction concept with a historically recognizable appearance and modern safety and operating systems.

## Current status

The public website, concept scope, visual references, development log and transparency pages are active. Detailed professional engineering and physical construction have **not** started. No construction date, budget or completion outcome is promised.

## Voluntary support

Support is optional and helps with ongoing concept-development costs. It is **not** an investment, equity purchase, loan, security, profit-sharing arrangement or purchase of goods/services. It creates no ownership, voting, repayment or guaranteed-return rights.
